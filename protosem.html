Week 10 — ASADI Specialized Session & SaaS Product Development

October 2026

Week 10 was a specialized learning session focused on ASADI, web development, LandChain and Landsmith integration, SaaS product development, and Large Language Models (LLMs). The four-day session progressed from individual problem-solving and website development to building an integrated SaaS-style web application.

Day 1 — Problem Statement & Individual Website Development

On Day 1, we were provided with a specific problem statement and were asked to independently develop a website based on the given requirement.

I worked on the website individually and followed a GitHub-based development workflow. Using the repository link provided during the session, I created a fork of the repository and used my forked repository to develop my solution.

After developing the required website, I pushed my implementation to GitHub. This gave me practical experience with repository forking, independent development, version control, and maintaining a project through GitHub.

Day 2 — LandChain & Landsmith Integration

Day 2 focused on understanding and working with LandChain and Landsmith integration.

The session introduced us to the integration workflow and how these technologies could be incorporated into a web application. I explored the required integration process and understood how different technologies can work together within a connected software system.

Day 3 & Day 4 — Integrated SaaS Product & LLM Learning

On Days 3 and 4, the individual problem statements were brought together to create a single website designed as a SaaS product.

Instead of maintaining separate websites for each problem statement, we worked towards combining the different requirements into one integrated platform. This helped me understand how multiple functionalities can be organized within a common product structure.

During these sessions, I also learned about Large Language Models (LLMs) and their role in modern software applications. This provided an introduction to how AI-based language models can be incorporated into intelligent and AI-assisted applications.

My Contribution

I independently worked on the assigned website, followed the GitHub fork-and-development workflow, explored the required LandChain and Landsmith integration, and contributed to the development process of the combined SaaS-style website. The session also helped me understand the transition from solving individual problem statements to thinking about a complete software product.
